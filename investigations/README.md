# Investigations

Kepler investigations fall into two categories.

## 1 Foundational investigations

These are guided observational studies intended to develop scientific intuition about the sky.

Examples include:

- creating a personal star map
- following the annual motion of the Sun
- tracking the Moon through a lunation
- observing planetary motion
- measuring elongation
- observing retrograde motion

## 2 Claim investigations

These challenge participants to evaluate historically important scientific claims.

Unlike foundational investigations, claim investigations intentionally avoid prescribing observational strategy or analytical method.

The investigator decides how to approach the problem.
