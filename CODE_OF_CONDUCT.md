# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors, maintainers, and participants pledge to make participation in the Kepler project a respectful, welcoming, and constructive experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We are committed to fostering an environment where scientific curiosity, careful reasoning, and respectful collaboration can thrive.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Treating others with respect and professionalism.
- Welcoming questions from participants of all experience levels.
- Providing constructive feedback.
- Being open to correction and alternative viewpoints.
- Giving appropriate credit for the work of others.
- Focusing discussions on ideas and evidence rather than individuals.
- Supporting reproducible and transparent scientific practice.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.
- Personal attacks or insulting language.
- Deliberate misinformation or misrepresentation of scientific work.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional scientific or educational setting.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, issues, or other interactions that violate this Code of Conduct.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories;
- issue discussions;
- pull requests;
- project documentation;
- community discussions;
- educational activities conducted under the Kepler project.

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please contact the project maintainers.

Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers will determine appropriate responses based on the nature and severity of the reported behavior.

Possible actions include:

- private discussion;
- formal warning;
- temporary restriction from participation;
- permanent removal from the project.
