# Calibration Campaign 001 — Terrestrial Geometry Validation

> **Objective**
>
> Validate the geometric behavior and repeatability of the Prototype 0.2 Kepler Cross-staff using stationary terrestrial targets of known dimensions.

## Scientific Question

Does the instrument produce repeatable measurements that agree with the geometry predicted by similar triangles?

This campaign is intended to evaluate the instrument itself rather than the observer.

---

## Equipment

- Calibrated Kepler Cross-staff
- Tape measure
- Pencil
- Notebook or electronic recording sheet
- Printed or hand-drawn calibration target
- Wall or other rigid mounting surface

---

## Calibration Target

Construct a calibration target consisting of several horizontal bars of accurately known width.

Suggested widths:

| Target ID | Width |
|-----------|------:|
| A | 0.5 in |
| B | 1 in |
| C | 2 in |
| D | 4 in |
| E | 8 in |

Mount the target securely so that the bars remain horizontal and flat.

Measure and record the actual width of each bar after construction.

---

## Experimental Setup

Measure a fixed observation distance from the target.

Suggested initial distance:

**10 ft (120 in)**

Mark the observer position on the floor so that every observation begins from the same location.

Maintain approximately the same eye position throughout the campaign.

---

## Observation Procedure

For each target width:

1. Select the fiducial that appears most appropriate.
2. Slide the crosspiece until the fiducial points exactly span the target bar.
3. Record:
   - Target ID
   - Fiducial used
   - Staff reading
4. Repeat the measurement **five times**, removing the instrument from the eye between observations.

Do not attempt to average mentally while measuring.

Each observation should be independent.

---

## Data to Record

For every observation record:

- Observation number
- Date
- Observer
- Instrument revision
- Fiducial used
- Target width
- Target distance
- Staff reading
- Notes

Example notes:

- uncertain alignment
- sleeve caught slightly
- observer repositioned
- difficult target
- poor lighting

---

## Analysis

After completing the observations:

- calculate the mean staff position for each target;
- calculate the standard deviation;
- compare observed geometry with the expected geometric relationship;
- identify systematic bias;
- identify outlying observations.

Do not discard observations without documenting the reason.

---

## Success Criteria

The campaign is considered successful if:

- repeated observations are internally consistent;
- no obvious mechanical problems are discovered;
- measured geometry agrees reasonably with expectation;
- instrument limitations are documented.

Repeatability is considered more important than perfect agreement during early prototype development.

---

## Deliverables

Archive the following with the instrument documentation:

- completed observation sheet;
- calibration target dimensions;
- photographs of the experimental setup;
- summary statistics;
- lessons learned;
- proposed improvements for the next prototype.

The resulting dataset becomes **Calibration Campaign 001** for this instrument revision and serves as the baseline against which future revisions may be compared.
